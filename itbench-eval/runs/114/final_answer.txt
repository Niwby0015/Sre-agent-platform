
Service restored and verified.

**Diagnosis:** The `product-catalog` Deployment, ReplicaSet and pod were all healthy (Running 5h, 0 restarts, Ready=True, memory ~16 MiB against a 32 MiB limit, correct image `demo:3.1.0-product-catalog`). The failure was one layer above: the ClusterIP **Service `product-catalog` did not exist**, so every caller resolving `product-catalog:8080` (frontend, recommendation, checkout, load-generator) hit a DNS/route dead end and reported errors — a total traffic blackhole with a perfectly healthy backend.

**How I confirmed it independently (not from a single tool)**:
- `kubectl_get_services` / `kubectl_get_endpoints`: 23 services, `product-catalog` absent from both — while every Deployment that legitimately has no Service (`accounting`, `fraud-detection` — both Kafka consumers) behaved as expected.
- Prometheus (kube-state-metrics) as a second source: `kube_service_info{service="product-catalog"}` → **NO DATA**, with control queries for `service="frontend"` and `service="recommendation"` returning series (so the matcher was sound, not silently broken).
- `kubectl_audit_selector_mismatch` was clean, ruling out a broken selector on an existing Service.
- Ruled out as noise, not cause: the `lookup opensearch ... no such host` log flood is cross-cutting OTLP-export noise from the collector (opensearch is disabled by Helm values) and appears identically in unrelated pods such as `recommendation`.

**Repair:** recreated the missing Service (ClusterIP, port/targetPort 8080) with the component label selector. No workload was deleted, scaled, or restarted; nothing outside `test-agent` was touched.

**Verification after the change:** `product-catalog` appears in `kubectl_get_services` (10.99.46.117:8080), `kubectl_get_endpoints` shows `product-catalog  1 READY / 0 NOT-READY / OK`, a fresh EndpointSlice `product-catalog-zhf99` exists, `kubectl_audit_selector_mismatch` reports none, all 26 pods remain Running with 0 new restarts, and there are no Warning events in the namespace.
